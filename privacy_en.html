Privacy Policy

1. General Information

This Privacy Policy explains how the website https://lakshmi-love.com (the "Website") collects, uses and protects information provided by visitors from various countries. Personal data is processed taking into account applicable provisions of the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection (ZZPL) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), to the extent applicable.

2. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for personal data collected via the Website is the owner of the "Lakshmi Love" Yorkshire Terrier kennel — private individual Ekaterina Miroshnichenko, located in Novi Sad, Republic of Serbia.

3. Personal Data We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through the contact form on the Website (if available on the Contacts page), we collect only the details you voluntarily provide:

  • Your name;
  • Your email address;
  • The content of your inquiry/message.

If you contact us directly via external messengers or social networks (such as Telegram, Instagram or Facebook), any data you share there is governed by the privacy policies of those respective platforms.

4. Purposes and Legal Bases of Processing

Your personal data is used exclusively to respond to your inquiries regarding puppies, reservations, acquisition, care advice, grooming or show preparation. Processing is based on your voluntary consent (provided by checking the consent box in the contact form) and the necessity to respond to your request. We do not send bulk promotional newsletters without a separate lawful basis.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to review your inquiry, communicate with you regarding your request, or until you withdraw your consent, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable law.

6. Sharing of Personal Data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third-party advertisers. To operate the Website and deliver contact form submissions, the following technical infrastructure providers are involved:

  • Cloudflare — used for domain management, content delivery, HTTPS encryption and security protection (may process technical connection data, such as IP addresses);
  • Web3Forms — used solely as a technical relay to forward contact form submissions to the Website owner.

7. International Data Transfers

Because the Website serves an international audience and uses global technical infrastructure (such as Cloudflare and form delivery services), data may be transmitted across borders strictly to the extent necessary to operate the Website and reply to your inquiry.

8. Cookies and Technical Technologies

We do not use advertising trackers, marketing pixels or third-party behavioral analytics. The Website and its technical infrastructure (including Cloudflare) may use strictly necessary technical cookies or browser storage solely for security, routing and remembering your selected language preference. The Contacts page also embeds an OpenStreetMap widget, which connects to OpenStreetMap servers when loaded.

9. User Rights

To the extent provided by applicable law (including ZZPL and GDPR), you have the right to:

  • request access to the personal data we hold about you;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten");
  • request restriction of or object to processing;
  • withdraw your consent at any time;
  • lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority (in Serbia, the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection, or your local EU data protection authority).

10. Data Security

Data transmitted via the Website is protected by HTTPS encryption. We take reasonable organizational steps to restrict access to received inquiries; however, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can guarantee absolute security.

11. Contact Information

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be published on this page with an updated effective date.
